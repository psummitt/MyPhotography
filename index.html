This website is dedicated to the photography I've taken over the years.

My dad bought me my first camera in the late 1960s. My first photographs were taken during a trip to Shiloh National Battlefield and I used B&W film. In college and in the Marines, I used Pentax cameras shooting mostly B&W. For a while in the mid-70s I owned an Olympus but I soon went back to Pentax and I have stayed with them until today.
